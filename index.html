How it works

From nothing to a ready worksheet in under a minute.

No templates, no searching, no reformatting. Just set the grade, pick a topic, and go.

STEP 01

Choose subject & grade

Select from Maths, English, Science, or Global Perspectives. Pick the grade level and topic — or let the app suggest what to practise based on past results.

STEP 02

AI generates the worksheet

Ten questions are written instantly — precisely calibrated to Cambridge International standards. A natural mix of multiple choice, short answer, true/false, and fill-in-the-blank.

STEP 03

Child earns XP & levels up

Print it or complete it online. Every worksheet scored adds XP, tracks weak areas, and moves your child closer to their next level — and their next achievement badge.

Q. Is Scholar's Quest free to use?
Yes — there's a free tier that includes one child profile and up to 5 worksheets per month. The paid plan unlocks unlimited worksheets, all four subjects, and online AI marking.
Q. What does the paid plan cost?
During the founding period, full family access is $10/month or $89/year — locked in permanently for early members. This rate increases once we open publicly.
Q. Which curriculum does it follow?
Scholar's Quest is built specifically around the Cambridge International Primary curriculum, covering Grades 1–7. Other curricula are planned for the roadmap — join the list to follow progress.
Q. Do children need their own device?
No. Worksheets can be printed on A4 and completed on paper. The online mode is optional and works on any shared device — children identify themselves with a 4-digit PIN.
Q. How does early access work?
Fill in the short form below. Early access is invitation-only for now — we review requests personally and email you an access link, usually within 48 hours.
Q. Can I use it for multiple children?
Yes. The paid plan covers your whole family — up to 4 child profiles, each with their own XP, badges, and history. A sibling leaderboard lets them compete for top spot.
